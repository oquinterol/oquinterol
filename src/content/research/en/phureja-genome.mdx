## Context

The ‘Criolla Colombia’ cultivar of _Solanum tuberosum_ L. Group Phureja motivates the creation of genomic resources for studying its biology. The thesis deposited at the National University of Colombia presents a genome assembly and a genome-scale metabolic model.

## From data to model

According to the institutional abstract, the work starts with PacBio HiFi reads, followed by assembly correction and assessment, scaffolding, gene prediction and annotation, and metabolic model reconstruction. The thesis documents the tools, parameters, and results of each stage; this site is not a substitute for that documentation.
