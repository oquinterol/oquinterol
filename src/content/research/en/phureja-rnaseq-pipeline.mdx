## Context

Pipelines make it possible to process large volumes of data faster and more reproducibly by automating the repetitive steps of handling RNA-Seq data. In crop improvement, this helps combine phenotypic and molecular markers to speed up selection. The thesis applies that approach to the agrigenomics of _Solanum tuberosum_ group Phureja.
